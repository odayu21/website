A new multimedia work for Orkest de Ereprijs "Auresona" will be premiered during Gaudeamus Festival. The work features trombonist Peter de Hoop, with electronics and real-time visuals. The composition is co-commissioned by Gaudeamus and Orkest de Ereprijs.

November 1 2026 @ Kyiv, Ukraine

Two multimedia works, "Velarium" (harpsichord, electronics, and real-time visuals) and "NoiseSample" (electronics and real-time visuals), will be performed in a concert presented in collaboration with harpsichordist Liubov Titarenko. The event will also present the premiere of "Orthorealm", a new installation version of the audiovisual work, built on a four-dimensional graphics system computed in real time and responding to the sound in the room. Time and location TBA.

January 31 2027 — 14:00 @ Yarai Noh Theatre (矢来能楽堂), Tokyo, Japan

A new commissioned work for Noh (shite and otsuzumi), tea ceremony, violin and cello will be premiered at Wa-You-Kyou-Ou (和洋響応), "Stillness and Resonance" (静寂と共鳴), a programme bringing together artists from different backgrounds. The piece is performed by Hino Naito (shite), Ryotaro Tsukuda (otsuzumi), Minoru Katayama (tea ceremony), Naoko Nakagawa (violin), and Hiroki Kadowaki (cello).
